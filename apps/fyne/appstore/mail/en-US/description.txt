Private, European mail over JMAP — no trackers, no ads.

• A fast inbox with threads, labels, filters and search.
• Attach files straight from Sytue Drive.
• Turn an email into a Calendar event.
• Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3). Sytue’s infrastructure is in the EU. The location of external services depends on their provider.
• Conversation threads with reading, reply and forward in one click.
• Folders, colour labels and search with operators (from:, has:attachment, is:unread…).
• Automatic rules that archive, label or move mail as it arrives.
• Scheduled send and “Undo send”.
• Signatures, sender aliases and an automatic vacation reply.
• Shared and delegated mailboxes with read and send permissions.
• Rich-text composer with attachments from Sytue Drive without duplicating files.
• Remote content blocked by default: nobody learns when you open an email.
• Signed mail (PGP and S/MIME) recognised and flagged; TLS in transit.
• Access from Thunderbird, Outlook, Apple Mail or your phone too, with app passwords.
• Recipient chips with the Contacts address book one click away, and a warning if you say «attached» without attaching.
