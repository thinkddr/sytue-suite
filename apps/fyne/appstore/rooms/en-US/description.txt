Channels, messages and team video calls in one place.

• Channels per team or project.
• A video call embedded in every channel.
• Mentions and notifications unified across the suite.
• Searchable history from anywhere in Sytue One.
• Public and private channels in categories; direct messages and groups.
• Threads, reactions, mentions, pins, saved messages and drafts.
• Formatting with an “Aa” bar and shortcuts: bold, italic, code, quotes, lists and links.
• Attachments, images, GIFs and custom emoji; files from Drive.
• Scheduled send, priority, read acknowledgement and “seen by”.
• Slash commands and cards: polls and assignable tasks.
• Video calls inside the channel: camera, screen, raised hand, chat and recording.
• Scheduled meetings and invite links.
• Bots, webhooks and integrations with other tools.
• Presence and custom status; per-channel notification settings.
• Search the history, and from across the suite.
• Real time over WebSocket, with reconnection and no lost messages.

Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3). Sytue’s infrastructure is in the EU. The location of external services depends on their provider.
