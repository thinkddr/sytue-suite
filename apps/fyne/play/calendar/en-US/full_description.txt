A CalDAV calendar wired into the rest of the suite.

• Day, week and month views.
• Recurring events and reminders.
• Create events straight from an email in Mail.
• Works with any CalDAV client.
• Month, week, day and agenda views, with a mini calendar and “Today”.
• Daily, weekly or monthly repeats with an end; edit just this one, this and following, or all.
• Reminders per event and notifications inside the suite.
• Invitations with a reply (going, maybe, no) and follow-up.
• “Find a slot” free among the attendees.
• Bookable rooms and resources with availability.
• Shared calendars with permissions, and the ones others share with you.
• Subscribe to external calendars (ICS) and keep several colour-coded calendars.
• Create an event from a Mail message or from a note.
• Works with any CalDAV client: Apple Calendar, Thunderbird and more.
• Duplicate an event or copy it to your calendar, and download it as .ics for anyone outside Sytue.

Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3). Sytue’s infrastructure is in the EU. The location of external services depends on their provider.
